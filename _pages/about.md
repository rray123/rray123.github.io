I am a final year PhD candidate at the Department of Statistics at Columbia University, where I am advised by [Cynthia Rush](https://www.columbia.edu/~cgr2130/) and [Marco Avella Medina](https://sites.google.com/site/marcoavellamedina/home?authuser=1). I received my BS/MA in Statistics and Data Science at Yale University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of statistical theory and machine learning (ML). Specifically, I focus on a few key areas:
* **Differential Privacy (DP), algorithmic fairness**: DP optimization, privacy-preserving analysis of heterogeneous data, design of DP sampling algorithms
* **Probabilistic machine learning, Bayesian asymptotics, approximate inference**: asymptotic guarantees for Bayesian methods that are robust to model misspecification. See my work [here](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.07900) and [here](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.09122) on posterior tempering, one such method
* **High-dimesional statistics**
* **Applications in clinical trial design, public health, and surveys**
